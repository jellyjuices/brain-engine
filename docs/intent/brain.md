# Intent: Brain

**Intent:** browse everything the owner knows, and open one full view of any subject that shows it all, over time. Brain is also where the owner deep dives on a subject when they need to.

## Problem

- What the owner knows about one subject, like a client, is spread across many notes.
- They cannot tell if something has always been bad or only lately.
- On flat days they feel they got nothing done, and has no proof otherwise.
- Related links are shown in several overlapping lists.

## Outcomes

1. Every subject opens one full view with: a short summary, current state, open to-dos, feelings, a dated timeline, and links.
2. The timeline shows every dated line about the subject, newest first: facts, feelings, done to-dos and their notes, sorted bits from journal entries.
3. Timeline filters sit behind one Filter button.
4. Feelings show in the owner's own phrases, grouped by side. Each side is named in their words. There is no number or rating.
5. People show as a row of faces. All other links are grouped by kind, each with why it is linked.
6. Person views add key facts (birthday, partner, what we do together) and gift ideas.
7. The Brain tab lets the owner browse areas, projects, people and events first, then every other type.
8. Decisions and Wins not tied to a goal live in Brain.
9. Looking back is on demand: the owner opens a timeline or asks Find. Nothing prompts them.
10. The owner can drill down from a subject view into more metrics and detail, all the way to the source files.
11. From any subject, the owner can open Connections to see how it links to other items (see [connections.md](connections.md)).

## User

The owner, before they meet someone, when they weigh a client, when they want the whole picture of one part of their life, or when they need to deep dive on one subject.

## Constraints

- One layout for every subject type. Goals and decisions have their own layouts (see [goals.md](goals.md), [decisions.md](decisions.md)).
- Parts with no content do not show.
- The description shows once. No section repeats it.
- Names only, never raw Markdown links.
- Edit, Move and Archive sit in a ⋯ menu.
- Feelings about a subject are dated `# Notes` lines marked `feeling`: `- 2026-11-12 · feeling · I think the people are nice but I am concerned about the amount of work and intensity.`
- A helper groups feeling quotes by side. The owner drags a quote to fix its side. A helper never labels their mood.
- Feelings about the owner themselves live in lenses (see [me-and-intake.md](me-and-intake.md)).
- Lines from other notes that link here show through backlinks. Nothing is copied.
- No scheduled review, no streaks, no missed-day counts.
- The archive holds every finished note and stays searchable.
