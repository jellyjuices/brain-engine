# Intake stages

Lenses: now, day, people, values, wellbeing, story, identity, future, profile. A lens note is made the first time an answer is saved to it. Mark a stage done with `brain intake done <stage>` (0 to 11, a name or a lens).

| Stage | Lens | Starts with | Branches to watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Set up | Explain the intake in three lines. | Privacy per lens. What the owner likes to be called, their pronouns, their words for their identities and people. The stop word. Which stage after the dump. Save in `me/settings.yaml`. |
| 1 | Brain dump | "What is on your mind? Type anything, one per line." | Walk the areas with the joggers below. Stop when two areas in a row give nothing new. |
| 2 | now | "What takes up the most room in your head these days?" Then rate each area 1 to 10. | "Why a 6 and not a 3?" "What would make it one point higher?" |
| 3 | day | "Walk me through yesterday, from waking up." | Per part of the day: where, with whom, energy 1 to 5. Screens, food, movement, sleep, work rhythm. |
| 4 | people | "Who are the people you are in touch with most?" | Family, partner or dating, friends, chosen family, clients, communities, helpers. Gives or takes energy, how often, anything unsaid. |
| 5 | now (context) | "What is the setup of your life: work, home, money, city?" | Work and income, money security, housing plans, neighbourhood, access to care, admin. Modern life module. |
| 6 | values | "When do you feel most like yourself?" | Pick and rank 5 from `resources/values-list.md`. What people come to them for. Which need is lowest: freedom, feeling capable, feeling close to others. |
| 7 | wellbeing | "How is your body doing these days? How is your head?" | Body module. Stress signs, moods, what calms them, how they cope, past help, what makes asking for help hard. |
| 8 | story | "If your life were a book, what would the chapters be?" | Per chapter: where, who mattered. A high point, a turning point. A low point only if they want it, as a headline. "What theme runs through it?" |
| 9 | identity | "Which parts of who you are matter most to you day to day?" | Identity module. "Where do two of these meet?" "Which part makes things easier or harder, and where?" |
| 10 | future | "Picture life a year from now, going well. What do you see?" Then 5 years. | What is in the way, what they keep putting off, what they fear, what they want to leave behind. |
| 11 | profile | Read all lens notes. Propose "the owner at a glance" and links between lenses. | The owner approves each line. Only links they agree with go in. Then they add `verified`. |

Story and identity come late on purpose: they go best once trust is there. "Across" branches can touch identity from stage 2 on.

## Modules

Coverage lists, not forms. At the end of a lens, ask only about what has not come up.

- **Identity.** Only the parts the owner says matter: age and generation; disability and neurodivergence; religion and spirituality; ethnic and racialized identity, heritage and languages; class growing up and now; sexual orientation and relationship style; Indigenous heritage; national origin and family migration; gender and what others expect of it. For each: "How does this make things easier or harder?" and "Where do you feel at home with it, and where do you hold it back?"
- **LGBTQ+ (only if the owner says it applies).** Strengths first: community, chosen family, where they feel fully themselves. Then, as much as they want: coming out, where they are out and not, unfair treatment (headlines only), expecting rejection or editing themselves, dating, care that fits them.
- **Body.** Family doctor or not; last check-up, dentist, eye exam. Sleep, energy, food, water, movement (link `areas/fitness.md`, do not repeat a fitness app). Alcohol, cannabis, nicotine. Pain, injuries, skin, things they put off. Sexual health and testing if they want it on record. Family health history. Stress in the body.
- **Modern life.** Screens, social media and news, and how they feel after. Solo work, isolation, the line between work and rest. Dating apps and online communities. How they use AI, and what the brain should never do. Cost of living where they live.

## Brain dump joggers

client-work: anyone waiting on you? business-admin: invoices, taxes, contracts? money: bills, subscriptions, big purchases? home: repairs, a move, landlord? health: appointments, check-ups? fitness: program, gear, recovery? career: portfolio, next contracts? love-life: dating, what you want in a partner, what is in the way? relationships: someone to call, something you owe? personal-projects: ideas that keep coming back? life-admin: documents, renewals, errands? rest-and-fun: trips, hobbies?

**Area check-in** (when an area feels heavy): `brain intake <area>` asks what is going on, what has a date, what worries them most, who else is involved, and what "handled" would look like.

## Rhythm after the first pass

No set habit. The owner adds when they like and looks back when they want to. Rerun Now and Context after a big change, such as a move or a new client.
